## Indefinite pronouns
**What are indefinite pronouns?**
Nouns can be a person, a place, a thing, intangible things

**Pronouns** *substitute those nouns*

Indefinite pronouns means *non-specific*
I would like to go *somewhere* cold in the winter season.

- Everybody/everyone **likes** to be appreciated
- Somebody called me ...
- Do you recommend anybody/anyone?

## Using Quantifiers

### A lot of
- *Positive* statements
- Countable and uncountable nouns
### **Much**
- *Negative* statements
- Questions
- Uncountable nouns only

## Many
- Mostly for *negative* statements
- Questions
- Countable nouns only
