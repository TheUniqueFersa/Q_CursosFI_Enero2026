# Cultural differences
## Schedules and time zones

![[Curso de Vocabulario en Inglés para el Trabajo - Timezone Quizz Answers.png]]
### **Aportación**
SUMMARY: Asynchronous work environments are when there are different coworkers in different parts of the word. We need to know when each coworker starts and finishes work. We also think about the cultural contexts of each zone.

- USA Time Zones: PST (Pacific Standard Time), CST (Central Standard Time), EST (Eastern Standard Time).
    
- USA Daylight Saving Time: All the clocks go forward one hour between March to November. EDT (Eastern Daylight Time): occurs during Daylight Savings. EST: the rest of the year.
    

If you send an email at 9 am to a zone that is 3 hours ahead of you: They receive your email at 12 am. If you are struggling with time zones and you need to have a meeting: google it.
