# ***♠Chess - Avoiding Bad Trades***

>ℹ Just because you can ***trade***, doesn't mean you have to trade
>
>✅ You should only ***trade*** if 
>- it improves your position or 
>- makes your opponent's position worst
>
>❌ It is usually a bad idea to trade one of your developed piece for an undeveloped piece. But this is not always true. For example a good reason to make a trade is *when you're attacking the king*
