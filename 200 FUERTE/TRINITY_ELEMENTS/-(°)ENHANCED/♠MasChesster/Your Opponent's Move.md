# ***♠Chess - Your Opponent's Move***
>Is as important as your own moves.
>Always verify what's behind each opponent's move

It’s important to pay attention to what your opponent is trying to do each turn.

>🗣 When a knight begins to jump, we want o carefully consider where it may land

![[Pasted image 20241216130644.png]]

The knight is not only attacking de pound, but also the king.
🔴 We can't move pound to c3, because that would let the enemy to double-attack the c2 square, therefore we would be losing either our rook or our queen

🟢 Instead, we would move knight to a3, so we can defend everything


>💡 Ask yourself every time your opponent makes a move, if my opponent had another move, what would he play?
>**Check every possible check**
